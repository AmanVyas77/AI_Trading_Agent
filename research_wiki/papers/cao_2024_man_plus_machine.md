# From Man vs. Machine to Man + Machine: The Art and AI of Stock Analyses

**Authors:** Cao, Jiang, Wang, Yang (2024) - Journal of Financial Economics
**Source:** SSRN 3840838

## Core Finding
Man + machine arrangement outperforms either human analysts or AI models alone in stock analysis quality.

## Relevance to Trading Agent
Directly motivates the hybrid architecture of Phase 4: LLM RAG assists research, but the quantitative ensemble model and human judgment remain in the loop. The LLM is a co-analyst, not a replacement.
